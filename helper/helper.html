Questions before you book?

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in opens at 2:00 pm and check-out is at 11:00 am. Ask us about early arrival or a late departure.

Can I change or cancel a booking?

Free changes up to 48 hours before arrival. After that, the first night may be charged.

Are pets welcome?

Small pets are welcome in Deluxe rooms and Villas. Please tell us in advance.

Do you offer airport transfers?

Yes. Share your flight details and we will arrange a driver.
